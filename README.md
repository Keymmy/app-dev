# 🎬 My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## 📖 About the Series

**Stranger Things** is a science fiction, horror, and mystery series created by the Duffer Brothers. The story takes place in a small town called Hawkins, where a group of friends discovers supernatural secrets and a mysterious alternate dimension called the *Upside Down*.

## ⭐ Main Characters

- **Eleven** – A girl with extraordinary supernatural powers.
- **Mike Wheeler** – A loyal friend who cares about his friends.
- **Dustin Henderson** – A funny and intelligent member of the group.
- **Lucas Sinclair** – A brave and dependable friend.
- **Will Byers** – A boy whose disappearance begins the main mystery.

## 🎭 Why I Like This Series

I like *Stranger Things* because it combines **friendship, adventure, mystery, and suspense**. The characters work together to solve problems and protect their loved ones. I also enjoy the unexpected twists and supernatural creatures that make every episode exciting.

> "Friends don't lie." — Eleven

## 📌 Interesting Facts

1. The series premiered on Netflix in **2016**.
2. It is set primarily in the **1980s**.
3. The story features secret experiments, supernatural powers, and parallel dimensions.
4. It combines horror, science fiction, and coming-of-age drama.
